01 / Introduction

Teaching as an invitation to question.

I am a Marion L. Brittain Postdoctoral Fellow at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where I teach technical communication. Trained in Victorian literature, I bring historical inquiry and close reading into conversations about contemporary media, design, and emerging technologies.

Across my courses, students learn to write with rhetorical intention, consider audiences beyond themselves, and evaluate the ethical implications of communication.

03 / Pedagogy

From knowing the conventions to understanding their consequences.

My pedagogy emphasizes rhetorical awareness, critical reflection, and iterative practice. I encourage students not only to produce effective writing but also to explain why particular forms, designs, and decisions serve particular people and purposes.

Through close reading, peer review, usability testing, and revision, students learn that communication is never neutral.

How does a document change when we ask not only whether it works, but for whom it works?Core teaching question
